# 🗝️ The Raja Saab: Shadows of the Labyrinth

> 🎮 **PLAY LIVE ONLINE NOW:**

A 2D top-down survival horror maze game inspired by *The Raja Saab*, built with HTML5, JavaScript, and Canvas, featuring a cinematic dark gothic aesthetic, dynamic torchlight and fog of war, BFS villain tracking AI, sequential audio pack, mobile touch controls, and companion follower train mechanics.

## 🔊 Audio Pack Features
- **`loop_on_start.mp3`**: Plays once on start screen at 40% volume.
- **`bgm.mp3`**: Atmospheric gothic horror background loop at 60% volume.
- **`on_see.mp3`**: Villain spotted cue with priority playback.
- **`run1.mp3`**: Footsteps/chase audio queued sequentially without overlap.
- **`die1.mp3`**: Defeat audio cue (*"Chachesadu baboi!"*).
- **`heroine3_sound.mp3`**: Telugu victory dialogue (*"Woah! Nenu vallala pichi danni anukunnava?"*) when Riddhi is rescued.

---

## 🕹️ Controls
- **PC**: WASD or Arrow Keys to steer. `Escape` or `⏸` to pause. `M` to mute.
- **Mobile**: Touch and drag anywhere on screen to guide Raja Saab.
- **Rescue**: Walk over keys to auto-collect. Walk up to cages to auto-rescue!
